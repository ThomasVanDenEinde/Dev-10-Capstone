TARGET QUESTIONS:
Visualize power creep, is it verifiable?
Visualize complexity creep is it verifiable?
What card characteristics are more valuable?
Do newer prints command higher prices for the same CMC?
Which color groups have the highest price volatility?

DATASET INFORMATION:
Both datasets were downloaded from MTGJSON.com
Both datasets were accessed on 6/19/2026
APA formated sources: All Files. (2025). Mtgjson.com. https://mtgjson.com/downloads/all-files/

ETL PROCESS:
Remove majority NA rows
Fill remaining NA’s
Change d-types
Merge by UUIDs between primary and secondary datasets
Remove any un-merged rows
Calc average price between all stores

TECHNOLOGIES USED:
Apache Airflow
Pandas
Pyspark
SQLAlchemy
Dash

CONCLUSIONS:
Both power and complexity creep are verifiable using linear or quadratic fits of power and complexity data over time
Low mana cost non creature cards are more valuable characteristics going off card price
print era alone does not strongly corrolate with price. The price fluctuates across mana costs for each era. 
4 colored cards have the highest price volatility because of one significant outlier. Aside that outlier, colorless and mon-colored cards are most volatile.
